# LAQN ingestion and multi-source station consolidation

21 November 2026 · UK AQ TEST technical assessment

## 1. Executive summary

LAQN is a moderate integration with substantial reusable code. TEST already has an ERG station discovery script, Python and Edge observation readers, a station polling selector and checkpoint table, and a disabled `laqn` network. The discovery and deploy paths were intentionally left inactive. The prototype needs source-authoritative timeseries discovery, correct network registration and quality handling, and integration with the current scheduler, write path and history workflow before activation. A canonical station schema redesign is unnecessary. [Ingest source](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-ingest/tree/ff9cf85/scripts/erg_laqn) · [Deferred discovery](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-ingest/blob/ff9cf85/.github/workflows/uk_aq_stations_daily.yml#L430-L436) · [Canonical network seed](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-schema/blob/9c6b132/schemas/ingest_db/uk_aq_reference_data.sql#L13-L26)

Register one independent `erg_laqn` connector under `laqn`. Ingest LAQN and AURN independently, including measurements from the same equipment when published by both. Connect confirmed duplicate site representations through `stations.match_id`. Preserve source identities, parallel instruments and dated measurement lineage. With both networks eligible and selected, the current Hex Map can display the fresher reading for each pollutant, with AURN winning equal-timestamp ties. Historical charts currently follow a single selected source. Continuous history across a source outage depends on that source's coverage today. An accepted future Hex policy already specifies how to choose the best-covered eligible single source for the requested range. [Canonical identity contract](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-system-docs/blob/812ff8d/system_docs/station_identity/contract.md) · [Current representative implementation](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/TEST-uk-aq.github.io/blob/7790229/shared/domain/canonical-current-rows.js) · [Future history selection](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-system-docs/blob/812ff8d/system_docs/website_ui/hex-map-canonical-history-source-contract.md)

Defra confirms **14 current sites and 17 all-time sites in its Automatic London Network**, an official subset shared by LAQN and AURN. Exact current LAQN and TEST coverage deltas remain open because the full source catalogue, its observation payloads and a current TEST inventory could not be retrieved in this session. There is enough evidence to identify the integration seams and the concrete work needed to close that gap. [Defra Automatic London Network](https://uk-air.defra.gov.uk/networks/network-info?view=aln)

### Matching and reconciliation

The Daily Stations resolver reads AURN records in the newest applicable UK-AIR register and associates SOS source rows via `sos_station_uk_air_refs`. Its other input connectors are `waqn`, `saqn` and `ni`. Their rules accept a unique exact published site code or, for a different code, one current AURN candidate within 50 m with identical coordinates or explicit corroboration from the official regional page. Ambiguous candidates are reported. Population uses stable, unique uppercase `station_matches.uk_air_ref`, with `match_method`, confidence and member evidence in metadata. A stored confidence of 1 describes the canonical UK-AIR reference, rather than a probabilistic score for each member. Legacy `match_key` is absent from the canonical clean shape and the mirror verifier rejects it. A station with no proven relationship retains its source ID and nullable `match_id`. The resolver reports conflicts with pre-existing assignments and preserves the source rows and histories. The Core mirror copies match rows before dependent stations with IDs intact. [Resolver](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-ingest/blob/ff9cf85/scripts/official_networks/canonical_site_resolver.py) · [Mirror](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-ingest/blob/ff9cf85/scripts/stations_daily/sync_obs_aqidb_uk_aq_core.py) · [Mirror verifier](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-schema/blob/9c6b132/schemas/obs_aqi_db/verify_clean_obs_aqi_db.sql)

LAQN, OpenAQ and Breathe London are outside the resolver's current connector set. The older candidate CSVs are investigation material. LAQN needs an evidence input to the existing resolver. Its instruments can share nearly identical coordinates and carry different site codes, so the devolved networks' coordinate-only acceptance branch is insufficient evidence for LAQN. Admit a shared code only when the official source records establish that it names the same station in the relevant period. Confirm different codes with an official cross-reference or documented device/inlet lineage. Keep ambiguous rows, Breathe London community nodes and other physically independent sensors separately discoverable even at identical coordinates. [Station identity contract](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-system-docs/blob/812ff8d/system_docs/station_identity/contract.md) · [Historical candidate file](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-ingest/blob/ff9cf85/archive/2026-02-08/network_info/LAQN/uk_aq_laqn_aurn_match_candidates.csv)

### What the Marylebone Road example proves

LondonAir and Defra independently identify `MY1` as Marylebone Road. Defra maps it to **`UKA00315`** and confirms AURN affiliation. This is strong site-level identity evidence. Defra lists hourly BAM PM10 and PM2.5 alongside historical FDMS fractions and other pollutant methods. LondonAir's `MY1` page labels PM10/PM2.5 TEOM and identifies its PM2.5 as non-reference-equivalent. LondonAir also publishes `MR8` (Marylebone Road BAM, open since January 2012) and `MY7` (FDMS PM10/PM2.5, closed December 2021). This demonstrates separate source codes and method periods at the same location. It does not establish that every `MR8` or `MY7` measurement is independent of the corresponding Defra instruments. Published dates and methods can differ and must be resolved at series and period level. No provider-wide official LAQN → UK-AIR crosswalk was found in the reviewed documentation. [Defra MY1 and UK-AIR ID](https://uk-air.defra.gov.uk/networks/site-info?site_id=MY1) · [LondonAir MY1](https://londonair.org.uk/london/asp/publicdetails.asp?MapType=Static&bulletin=&details=&la_id=&mapview=all&network=LAQN&postcode=&region=12&site=MY1) · [LondonAir MR8](https://londonair.org.uk/london/asp/publicdetails.asp?MapType=Static&VenueCode=&bulletin=&la_id=&postcode=&region=11&site=MR8) · [LondonAir MY7](https://www.londonair.org.uk/london/asp/publicdetails.asp?MapType=Static&VenueCode=&bulletin=&la_id=0&postcode=&region=0&site=MY7)

AURN and LAQN can redistribute the same instrument's output, supply different parallel instruments, and expose different periods or pollutant definitions at one location. Compare paired UTC hours only after confirming site code, pollutant, unit, method, integration interval, grade, correction and ratification. A match at the site level never certifies equality of concentrations. Shared equipment and telemetry can still fail upstream of both publishers. [Defra ALN](https://uk-air.defra.gov.uk/networks/network-info?view=aln) · [Future matched-network history audit](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-system-docs/blob/812ff8d/system_docs/station_identity/dual-network-history-audit-contract.md)

## 6. Recommended architecture

1. **One connector, one reference owner.** Seed `erg_laqn` with the existing `laqn` network as its default, retaining independent ingest, polling, general public display and live-map controls. Daily Stations should acquire and validate the official register and site/species, populate the existing `laqn_site_register` with source evidence, and upsert source stations and actual discovered series before matching, geography and mirroring. Preserve initial metadata once and merge subsequent owned fields without clobbering geographic or other enrichment. Keep closed records for history. Explicit closure, confirmed outage, missing catalogue entry and relocation have different meanings. A partial or empty source response never establishes retirement. [Network mirror contract](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-system-docs/blob/812ff8d/system_docs/ingest/network_catalogue_mirror_contract.md) · [Metadata ownership](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-system-docs/blob/812ff8d/system_docs/ingest/station_metadata_ownership_contract.md)
2. **Stable, dated source series.** Use ERG's actual source code and pollutant/series identity. Retain existing `SiteCode:SpeciesCode` where it identifies one series. Keep parallel instruments separately addressable using genuine source distinctions. Record units, method, grade, comparable intervals and any network affiliations from the source. Where an analyser replacement preserves a publisher's definition and comparability, retain the series with dated method lineage. When a code is reused or an instrument changes the measurement meaning, preserve and end the old identity and register the new one. A confirmed relocation needs a dated station/series decision before changing the coordinates of a historical identity. Use the established source-specific continuity process only for proven compatible replacements. [Core series schema](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-schema/blob/9c6b132/schemas/ingest_db/uk_aq_core_schema.sql#L1103-L1149) · [History area](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-system-docs/blob/812ff8d/system_docs/r2_history/README.md)
3. **Reuse the scheduled official-network runtime.** Adapt the existing Python ERG acquisition and date handling to the shared official-network Cloud Run service, compact pooled write path, Pub/Sub/ObsAQIDB delivery and Ingest Scheduler. Add ERG as a source adapter with its own profile, preserving the behaviour of existing official profiles. This is the proposed single runtime. The prototype Edge handler and generic dispatcher identify useful prior work, but adding the Edge back to deployment would also require restoring a compatible dispatch/RPC path and fixing redundant catalogue reads and per-poll reference upserts. Keep historical bulk reads out of the short scheduled runtime. Use one request per eligible site if the bounded whole-site endpoint returns fully identifiable, correctly flagged series more efficiently than individual species requests. Verify equivalence first. [Official network implementation](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-ingest/tree/ff9cf85/scripts/official_networks) · [Official Cloud Run deployment](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-ingest/blob/ff9cf85/.github/workflows/uk_aq_official_networks_cloud_run_deploy.yml) · [Supabase log budget](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-system-docs/blob/812ff8d/system_docs/supabase_log_ingestion/contract.md)
4. **Separate polling fairness from observation progress.** Reuse the existing station checkpoint as the scheduling attempt cursor, adapted to the chosen runtime. Bound lookback and coverage catch-up separately. A retryable failure or partial batch must retain the unfinished series/window, leave the previous good value available and report partial/failed outcomes. Recover from accepted durable observations and transport state, never from attempt time alone. Use bounded overlap for repeated quality revisions, idempotent source-key writes and a contracted recovery path for downstream delivery. The legacy `start_from_latest` can expand a request arbitrarily backwards and latest-value writes only advance on a later timestamp. Handle revisions to the same latest hour and invalidations through established reconciliation instead of leaving a formerly provisional value current. Size the schedule so the full active set fits within the accepted freshness target. At the old default of 10 stations per hour, the historical 80 open-code example would take eight hours per pass even before retries. [Legacy dispatcher](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-ingest/blob/ff9cf85/supabase/functions/uk_aq_dispatch_polls/index.ts) · [Prototype Edge polling loop](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-ingest/blob/ff9cf85/supabase/functions/ingest_erg_laqn/index.ts#L1998-L2198) · [Snapshot reconciliation contract](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-system-docs/blob/812ff8d/system_docs/latest_snapshot/integrity_reconciliation.md)

Persist evidence in existing register and canonical/member metadata. Keep exact producer labels and source rows through the mirror and snapshot. Apply existing live-map filters and freshness ranking. Add required ERG/Imperial LondonAir attribution via the existing footer definition and network catalogue path. Keep `breathelondon` unified and preserve SOS, Nodes and Communities ownership. A separate LAQN-specific frontend or ranking implementation would duplicate existing presentation policy. [Resolver](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-ingest/blob/ff9cf85/scripts/official_networks/canonical_site_resolver.py) · [Website attribution contract](https://github.com/TEST-uk-aq/uk-aq-system-docs/blob/812ff8d/system_docs/website_ui/site-footer-attribution-contract.md)

## 8. Risks and unresolved decisions

- **Coverage and affiliation.** Full official station/series responses and current TEST data are required to reconcile the advertised network scale, the scoped historical extract and AURN subset. This includes instrument aliases, relocations and code reuse.
- **Missing and revised data.** The archived empty-value case proves a parser defect in the prototype's handling of source data. A source-only flag or effective method interval might require combining a documented CSV/download product with API observations. The format's source authority must be established before ranking on quality or accepting historical Health coverage.
- **Instrument equivalence and conflict.** Marylebone Road's method and code differences demonstrate why a site match must be kept distinct from a measurement equivalence decision. In particular, proxy independence can still share an instrument, logger or quality process. Keep exact evidence and preserve differences.
- **Polling and history capacity.** The legacy ten-station rotation and uncapped `start_from_latest` design can exceed freshness or time budgets. Confirm hourly publication latency, limits, admitted windows, sustained full sweeps and downstream delivery before public enablement. The future history selector can improve coverage only where one eligible source covers the period.
- **Consumer readiness.** Sensors' retired-RPC dependency requires deployed verification. Eligibility also requires geography and network flags, so a correctly registered LAQN station may be absent from public snapshots. As the Health contract requires an accepted LondonAir gap assessment, the missing TEST join is an open dependency for Health use.

## 9. Final recommendation

Implement LAQN first as an independently polled, disabled-by-default TEST connector on the existing official-network ingest path. Complete the read-only inventory and measurement-lineage comparison, then bring up targeted PM2.5, PM10 and NO₂ ingestion and source-authenticated history. Extend the existing canonical resolver only for proven duplicate representations. Use the existing freshness and tie-breaking policy for map resilience, and validate how history and AQI behave when the live source changes. If one-source historical selection limits the intended rollout, extend the already accepted coverage-based Hex policy after LAQN history is verified. Keep both publishers and all genuine independent instruments available.
